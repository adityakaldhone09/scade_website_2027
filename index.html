Institutional Profile

Bridging Theory & Practical Defense

SCADE is the official technical organization dedicated to cultivating expertise in cyber security architectures, defensive countermeasures, and data science methodologies at GHRCEM.

Guided by Department Head Dr. Deepika Ajalkar, Activity Coordinator Prof. Swati Gajarlewar, and Club Coordinator Prof. Gayatri Deshmukh, SCADE fosters a vibrant technical ecosystem where students collaborate on real-world projects, compete in hackathons, and connect with industry leaders.

Our Core Activities

What We Do

Practical domains through which SCADE empowers engineering students.

Technical Workshops

Masterclasses and bootcamps led by industry guest experts covering version control, Linux environments, and defensive security workflows.

Competitions & CTFs

Challenging quiz sprints, cryptographic treasure hunts, and algorithmic problem-solving competitions testing speed and logic.

Student Projects & Patents

Mentoring capstone engineering innovations, guiding students toward patent-worthy prototypes and research publications.

Cyber Defense Labs

Hands-on practice exploring network packet captures, port reconnaissance, and ethical vulnerability disclosure.

Data Intelligence

Training on structured business intelligence tools, machine learning pipelines, and analytics dashboards for actionable insights.

Community & Mentorship

Fostering peer collaboration, celebrating faculty dedication, and building lasting professional networks across academic years.
