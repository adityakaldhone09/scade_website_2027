Teacher's Day Celebration

A special gathering organized by the SCADE Club to celebrate and honor the mentorship, guidance, and dedication of the GHRCEM faculty.

Date: 25/09/2024 Venue: Department of CSE, GHRCEM Category: Faculty Gratitude & Community Coordination: Prof. Swati Gajarlewar

The SCADE Club, under the Department of CSE (Cyber Security & Data Science), successfully organized an inspiring event on the occasion of Teacher's Day to express sincere student gratitude for the ceaseless dedication, mentorship, and support of their educators. The event provided a meaningful occasion to acknowledge the vital role faculty members play in shaping academic and professional trajectories.

Strengthening Mentorship Bonds

The celebration featured a variety of engaging team games organized especially for the faculty, fostering lighthearted camaraderie and building deeper student-mentor relationships beyond formal classroom interactions.

The atmosphere was filled with energy and laughter as teachers enthusiastically participated in interactive challenges. The celebration concluded on a deeply appreciative note, with students sharing heartfelt tributes highlighting the invaluable impact of the faculty's guidance.

As a gesture of esteem and admiration, custom mementos were presented to the teachers. The session was coordinated by Prof. Swati Gajarlewar as the Activity Coordinator, under the guidance of Dr. Deepika Ajalkar (HOD, CSE - Cyber Security & Data Science) and Dr. R D Kharadkar (Campus Director, GHRCEM).
