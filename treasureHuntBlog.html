Campus Technical Treasure Hunt

A fast-paced algorithmic sprint blending cryptographic clue deciphering, logical puzzles, and physical campus exploration.

Venue: GHRCEM Campus, Pune Category: Cryptography & Algorithmic Sprint Format: Multi-team Relay Organizer: SCADE Event Committee

The Campus Technical Treasure Hunt is one of SCADE's flagship problem-solving challenges at GHRCEM, Pune. Designed to bridge abstract theoretical concepts with practical detective logic, the event challenged student teams across a series of cryptographic and computational stages scattered across the campus grounds.

Cipher Breaking & Algorithmic Clues

Participating teams were issued encrypted data packets requiring knowledge of classical ciphers (Substitution, Caesar, Vigenère), hash comparisons (MD5, SHA-256), and binary/hex conversion to uncover physical waypoint coordinates.

Each physical checkpoint presented an algorithmic puzzle or debugging riddle that had to be solved collaboratively within strict time constraints before the clue to the subsequent checkpoint was revealed. The challenge demanded rigorous teamwork, speed, and analytical endurance under pressure.

The event concluded with an exhilarating sprint to the final destination, with winning teams felicitated for their exceptional code-breaking and strategic agility. The initiative exemplified SCADE's mission: transforming technical education into an engaging, collaborative, and memorable student experience.
