Zero Day: Newbie Unveil

Society of Cyber Security Advancements & Data Science Excellence — The Official Foundational Launch at GHRCEM, Pune.

Date: 12/08/2024 Venue: GHRCEM Auditorium, Pune Category: Inauguration & Technical Symposium Attendance: 80+ Students & Faculty

The Zero Day: Newbie Unveil event marked a significant milestone for SCADE at GHRCEM, Pune, bringing together students and faculty for an inspiring day. The event commenced with a warm welcome for Masira Ma'am and Dr. Deepika Ajalkar Ma'am, who inaugurated the occasion. This was followed by a token of appreciation from Masira Ma'am to Deepika Ma'am, reflecting the department's strong camaraderie and academic vision.

Industry Training Alliances

Dr. Deepika Ajalkar delivered an insightful keynote emphasizing practical knowledge to meet modern industry standards. She encouraged Cyber Security students to master practical tools like Kali Linux and officially announced partnerships with industry leaders like Kali.io and Wisdom Sprouts.

Mishra Sir then addressed the gathering on the crucial significance of student internships, patents, and original research. He stressed the imperative to avoid copyright infringement and guided students toward publishing impactful research papers in peer-reviewed technical journals.

Next, Mr. Pranit Siddam, the founding lead of SCADE GHRCEM, took his post with an inspiring speech outlining the society's roadmap across technical domains. Following this, Mr. Tousif Tamboli, the Tech Lead, showcased the official SCADE GHRCEM website, explaining its technical architecture, code structure, and development stack.

The event was hosted by Tejashvi Tiwari and Abhay Sonkusare, and featured an engaging technical quiz competition that tested students across computer networks, cryptography, and logic. With around 80 students in attendance and coordinated smoothly by Prof. Swati Gajarlewar, the event established a robust foundation for SCADE's long-term initiatives.
