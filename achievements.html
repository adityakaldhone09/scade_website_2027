Milestones

Institutional Highlights & Industry Alliances

Strategic accomplishments advancing cybersecurity education and student research.

Industry Partnership

Kali.io & Wisdom Sprouts Collaboration

Announced during the inaugural ZeroDay symposium by HOD Dr. Deepika Ajalkar. Partnerships establish direct pathways for students to access practical tools, industrial training, and certified cybersecurity curricula.

Open Source

GitHub Bootcamp & Developer Enablement

Conducted an intensive version control masterclass hosted by guest expert Ms. Sanika Kotigre, enabling 80+ engineering students to adopt industry-standard Git branching, pull requests, and collaborative repository workflows.

Intellectual Property

Patents & Research Paper Initiatives

Established a formalized support framework under Mishra Sir and Dr. Deepika Ajalkar, encouraging students to submit original research papers, avoid copyright infringement, and convert capstone projects into patent-worthy innovations.

Club Milestone

Foundational Launch of SCADE

Successfully launched the Society of Cyber Security Advancements & Data Science Excellence, inaugurating the club with faculty dignity, student leadership induction, and widespread departmental participation.

Competition

ZeroDay Technical Quiz Challenge

Conducted an engaging technical quiz competition hosted by Tejashvi Tiwari and Abhay Sonkusare, testing rapid problem-solving and domain knowledge among 80+ participating computer engineering students.

Department Culture

Faculty Mentorship & Recognition

Strengthened student-faculty coordination through the Teacher's Day Gratitude initiative and continuous project mentorship sessions overseen by Prof. Swati Gajarlewar and Prof. Gayatri Deshmukh.
