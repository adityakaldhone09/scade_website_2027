About SCADE Club

Society of Cyber Security Advancements & Data Science Excellence — Empowering innovation, technical rigor, and student leadership at GHRCEM, Pune.

Our Identity

Pioneering Cyber Defense & Intelligent Analytics

The SCADE Club (Society of Cyber Security Advancements & Data Science Excellence) is the premier technical body operating under the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security & Data Science) at G H Raisoni College of Engineering and Management (GHRCEM), Pune.

Founded with the conviction that future engineering challenges demand deep technical competence, SCADE bridges the gap between academic curricula and rapidly evolving industrial disciplines. Through structured bootcamps, hands-on vulnerability laboratories, competitive hackathons, and collaborative research initiatives, SCADE equips students with real-world technical acumen.

Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Formulated to cultivate academic excellence, technical prowess, and professional integrity.

Our Vision

To establish a nationally recognized student center of excellence in cybersecurity defense and data science innovation, fostering skilled practitioners capable of securing critical cyber infrastructure and deriving impactful intelligence from complex data.

Our Mission

At SCADE GHRCEM, our mission is to foster a rigorous culture of continuous learning, technical exploration, and interdisciplinary collaboration in the domains of cybersecurity and data science through hands-on workshops, competitive environments, and expert mentorship.

Core Objectives

  • Deliver practical training in Kali Linux, Wireshark, Python, and SQL.
  • Facilitate original student projects, research publications, and patents.
  • Host competitive CTF (Capture the Flag) and data analytics hackathons.
  • Connect students with industry veterans, alumni, and research bodies.

Chronology

The SCADE Journey

Key milestones achieved since the club's establishment.

Aug 2024
Inauguration: ZeroDay — Newbie Unveil

The official grand launch of SCADE at GHRCEM Pune. Inaugurated by Masira Ma'am, Dr. Deepika Ajalkar, and Prof. Swati Gajarlewar. Introduced founding student leadership, established partnerships with Kali.io and Wisdom Sprouts, and conducted technical quizzes for over 80 attendees.

Sep 2024
GitHub Bootcamp: Beginner to Expert

Intensive technical seminar conducted by expert guest speaker Ms. Sanika Kotigre. Provided hands-on training on Git architecture, branch management, collaborative version control, and open-source contribution workflows.

Sep 2024
Teacher's Day & Academic Gratitude Ceremony

Commemorative event dedicated to faculty members, featuring interactive team games, mementos of gratitude, and strengthening student-faculty mentorship across the department.

Annual
Technical Treasure Hunt & Problem Solving Sprint

Campus-wide technical scavenger and problem-solving competition challenging students across cipher decryption, logical debugging, and collaborative algorithmic thinking.
