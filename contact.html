Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifications regarding participation, membership, and club offerings.

All technical programs, guest seminars, and workshops are open to any motivated student interested in Cybersecurity, Ethical Hacking, Machine Learning, and Data Science at GHRCEM. Prior membership is not mandatory to attend open events.

Members gain hands-on technical competencies in tools like Kali Linux, Wireshark, Python, and Power BI; receive guidance on student patents and research publications; network with industry speakers; and gain recognized leadership experience in organizing university-scale tech events.

You can regularly check the Events Hub on this website, follow our official announcements on our Linktree (linktr.ee/scadeghrcem), or sign up through our member portal.
