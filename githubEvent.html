GitHub Bootcamp: Beginner to Expert

Hands-on technical workshop on distributed version control, collaborative workflows, and open-source contribution with guest speaker Ms. Sanika Kotigre.

Date: 25/09/2024 Speaker: Ms. Sanika Kotigre Category: Version Control Masterclass Attendance: 80+ Engineering Students

The SCADE Club, under the Department of CSE (Cyber Security & Data Science) at GHRCEM Pune, organized an intensive seminar titled "GitHub Bootcamp: Beginner to Expert". The featured guest speaker for the session was Ms. Sanika Kotigre, a seasoned practitioner in open-source collaboration and enterprise Git workflows.

Practical Hands-On Curricula

Students were led through live terminal exercises covering essential Git commands, staging trees, conflict resolution during merges, and strategic branch management models adopted by top engineering teams.

The session aimed to educate students on the pivotal role of Git and GitHub in modern software development, security audits, and collaborative engineering environments. Ms. Kotigre demonstrated how version control empowers teams to audit commit histories, maintain cryptographic signing on commits, and streamline continuous integration pipelines.

The workshop featured dynamic interactive Q&A segments where students received direct assistance debugging local merge issues, authenticating with SSH keys, and contributing effectively to public open-source codebases.

A total of 80 students attended and actively benefited from the masterclass. The session was coordinated by Prof. Swati Gajarlewar (Activity Coordinator) under the strategic guidance of Dr. Deepika Ajalkar (HOD, CSE - Cyber Security & Data Science) and Dr. R D Kharadkar (Campus Director, GHRCEM).
